By the Time I Get to Phoenix (five year anniversary)

To commemorate the five year anniversary of the release of our final album as Injury Reserve, By the Time I Get to Phoen ix, we are releasing a limited series of vinyl. This edition features two new unreleased bonus tracks titled "Tally Em Up" and "Altitude". It is pressed on two color ripple effect vinyl, includes a new 24x24" cover art poster, a printed artwork innersleeve, and a very nice metallic chrome edit of the back cover with tonal details printed on top.
